💾 SAVE Menu Pages Explained (Pages 1–6)

      

        
          
Save PageKnobs (K1 / K2 / K3 / K4)Plain-Language Explanation

        
        
          
1/6 PROJECTSLOT / LOAD / SAVE / NEWFull Song / Project Slots (S01–S16): Saves or loads all 4 tracks, their sequences, sounds, FX settings, and tempo together. Turn K2 to load, K3 to save, or K4 to initialize a clean project.

          
2/6 TOOLSTRACK / SEQ / SOUND / LOCKSQuick-Clear Utilities: Resets parts of the current track without wiping the whole song. TRACK resets the whole track; SEQ clears the step pattern; SOUND resets the synth patch to default; LOCKS removes step automation locks.

          
3/6 USERSLOT / LOAD / SAVE / CLR32 User Patch Slots (U01–U32): Your personal sound bank! Save your custom synth patch on the active track into one of 32 flash slots (K3), load one into the current track (K2), or clear a slot (K4).

          
4/6 PRESETSPATCH / ENG / FAV / LISTPreset Browser & Favorites: K1 scrolls presets one-by-one; K2 jumps by Synth Engine; K3 marks/unmarks a preset as a Favorite (★); K4 filters the browser by category tag (ALL, FAV, USER, BASS, LEAD, PAD, etc.). Leaving this page automatically restores full preset access on tracks.

          
5/6 PHRASESSLOT / LOAD / SAVE / CLRPattern / Sequence Bank (P01–P16): Saves or loads a 16-step sequence (notes + motion locks) independently of the project so you can reuse riffs across songs.

          
6/6 INIT+SAVE / LOAD / CLR / —Custom Default Boot State: Saves your current project layout as the personal default template that loads whenever you initialize a new project.

        
      

    


    

      
🎛️ Plain-Language Parameter & Engine Glossary

      

        
          
Term / LabelWhere FoundPlain-Language Meaning

        
        
          
SCL & QNT (OFF, SNAP, WHITE, SEQ)SCL Button (Scale Page)Scale Quantization: Selecting any scale automatically enables SNAP. SNAP rounds out-of-scale notes to the nearest note in the chosen scale; WHITE maps consecutive scale degrees across the white keys so every white key is in-key; SEQ quantizes recorded sequencer notes to the scale while leaving live keys chromatic.

          
Resampling & USR1..3Workbench Top Bar & Editor & SamplesResampling & Custom Samples: Because the physical FM-1 has no audio input jack, custom audio enters slots USR1..3 over USB SysEx via the Editor & Samples tab, or by clicking ● Resample Audio in the top bar while playing the emulator to capture a WAV file of your loop and load it into USR1..3 for SLICE, SCRUB, WAVETABLE, or GRAIN.

          
SLCS (Slices 1–16)SLICE Engine (Edit 1 K2)Slice Count: Sets the number of slices (1 to 16) across the full sample waveform. Changing SLCS automatically recalculates slice points to snap to transients across the entire sample. Each white key triggers one slice with its own live playhead.

          
POS, MOTOR, INERT, FRICSCRUB EngineTape / Platter Scrubbing: POS scrubs the tape playhead directly; MOTOR sets continuous forward/reverse reel speed (-3.0x to +3.0x); INERT controls platter mass/momentum (higher = heavier, more elastic lag); FRIC sets brake friction when the motor stops.

          
DUCK, TRIG, PUMP, RELS, FXDKFX Menu (Send 1 & Duck Page)Sidechain Ducking Compressor: Replaces the old send distortion. Ducks tracks and/or master FX whenever the selected drum trigger hits. TRIG selects KICK (default), K+SNR, K+TOM, or ALL; PUMP sets threshold/ratio depth; RELS sets release recovery speed; FXDK ducks the global Chorus/Delay/Reverb tails.

          
TAPE DELAY (TIME, FB, TAPE, CROSS)FX Menu (Delay Page)Continuous Tape Echo: Smooth continuous delay time (5–720 ms) with gentle analog tape-transport inertia for pitch-bent dub sweeps without excessive wobble. TAPE increases both Wow and Flutter (with Wow scaled 3× relative to Flutter).

          
CHORUS (RATE, DEPTH, TONE, VOICES)FX Menu (Chorus Page)Multi-Voice Chorus: TONE shapes warmth vs. shimmer; VOICES selects 1 to 4 detuned quadrature chorus voices.

          
REVERB (TYPE, TIME, DAMP, TONE)FX Menu (Reverb Page)Stereo Reverb: TONE tilts the reverb tank from dark/warm (negative) to bright/hard (positive).

          
Insert FX (Track FX)FX Menu (Insert Page)Per-Track Insert Effect: Each of the 4 tracks (T1..T3, DRUM) has its own dedicated Insert FX processor (Overdrive, Bitcrush, Decimator, Filter, Phaser, Tremolo, RingMod, Compressor, Comb) before feeding the 4 global Send buses.
